About "Do You Love Me?" — Advance Options

"Do You Love Me?" is a free, no-signup, no-app love prank and couple-activity website. The "Advance Options" section adds five extra relationship mini-tools on top of the original Yes/No prank: A & Z Edit — Advance, Custom Love Prank, Two-Way Love Decoder, Time Capsule Confession, and Game (Sync Pulse). Everything runs entirely inside the browser — there is no backend server, no account system, no database, and no data collection. All data (names, questions, answers, theme, music, and unlock dates) is encoded directly into the shareable link's URL, so the link itself is the only place the data ever exists.

99.99% Data Safe — Guaranteed

Nothing typed into this site is ever sent to, stored on, or logged by any server. Every feature works by encoding the data into the URL hash (the part after the # symbol), which browsers never transmit to a server during a normal page load. The generated link is the only "storage" that exists — copying or sharing that link is the only way the data persists. If the link is lost, the data is gone permanently, because there is nothing stored anywhere else to recover it from.

The Five Advance Features

1. A & Z Edit — Advance

The complete builder that combines every other feature into one link. Pick a color theme (pink, gold, emerald, ruby, sunset, or ocean), write a custom prank question or pick a preset quote, set a Two-Way Love Decoder hint with a password-protected secret answer and a message revealed on success, hide a Time Capsule Confession note that unlocks at a date and time you choose, and pick background music (three built-in tracks or a custom YouTube link). Tapping "Generate & Copy Link" combines all of it into a single shareable link.

2. Custom Love Prank

Add your name (optional) and write your own custom question (optional, default is "Do you love me?"). This generates a shareable link that opens a playful Yes/No prank page: the "No" button moves away from the tap so it can never be pressed, and tapping "Yes" opens a result page confirming the answer.

3. Two-Way Love Decoder

A compatibility quiz for two people, built around 6 fixed questions (e.g. "When do you miss me the most?", "What's my favorite food?"). Person A answers all 6 questions first and can skip any question or go back to edit a previous answer before generating the link. A link is generated and sent to Person B. Person B answers the same 6 questions on their own device — the site never shows Person B what Person A wrote — and Person B can also skip questions or revisit earlier answers before submitting. Once both people have answered, a compatibility match percentage is calculated and revealed to both. The full question-by-question comparison is visible only to the two original participants (recognized automatically on the device that generated the result); anyone else who opens a shared result link sees a limited preview only (2 sample questions plus the overall match percentage), not the full breakdown.

4. Time Capsule Confession

Write a message today that stays locked and hidden until a future date and time you choose (quick presets: 1 day, 1 week, 1 month — or a fully custom date). Anyone who opens the link before the unlock time sees a live countdown timer instead of the message. Once the unlock time passes, the message is revealed automatically with a confetti animation.

5. Game (Sync Pulse)

A two-person, same-device mini-game. Two players share one phone, each tapping their own half of the screen after a 3-2-Start countdown. The app measures the time difference between the two taps in milliseconds and converts it into a synchronization percentage over 2 rounds, then averages the results into a final sync score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is any data sent to a server or stored in a database?

No. There is no backend, no database, and no analytics collection of the content entered into any feature. All data (names, custom questions, quiz answers, capsule messages, unlock dates) is encoded into the URL hash on the sender's device and decoded again on the receiver's device when they open the link — the content never passes through or touches a server.

Do I need to create an account or download an app?

No account, login, email, phone number, or signup is required for any feature. No app store download is needed either — the site itself can optionally be installed to a phone or desktop home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for quicker access and offline viewing of already-visited pages, but this is entirely optional.

Can I skip a question or change a previous answer in the Two-Way Love Decoder?

Yes. While answering the 6 questions (for either person), a "Skip" option moves to the next question without requiring an answer, and a "Previous" option goes back to review or edit an answer already given, before the final link is generated or submitted.

Does the Two-Way Love Decoder link work if the other person opens it on a different phone or device?

Yes. The link is fully self-contained — all of Person A's answers are encoded directly inside the URL itself, so Person B can open the exact same link on any device, any browser, with no login and no shared account, and the site will correctly load Person A's data and show the questions for Person B to answer.

What happens if the shared link looks broken or shows "Link Not Valid"?

This can happen if the link was cut off while copying or pasting (for example, a messaging app inserting a line break in the middle of the long link) so it arrives incomplete. Always use the in-app "Copy Link" or "Share" button rather than manually selecting the link text, and paste the full link exactly as copied into the browser's address bar.

Is this site free to use?

Yes, every feature on "Do You Love Me?" — the original Yes/No prank and all four Advance Options — is completely free with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden paywalls.
