Open "Advance Options" from this site to unlock five extra relationship mini-tools. Each one gives you a shareable link you can send to another person — no signup or app download needed for any of them.

The all-in-one builder. 2. Custom Love Prank

Add your own name and write your own custom question instead of the default "Do you love me?". Generate a link and send it — when the other person taps Yes, your name and question are revealed to them.

A compatibility quiz for two people. You answer a set of fun questions and send the link to someone; they answer the same questions on their own device without seeing your answers first. Once both of you have answered, a compatibility match percentage is revealed to both of you.

4. Time Capsule Confession

Write a message today and lock it until a future date and time you pick. Anyone who opens the link early only sees a countdown timer — the message reveals itself automatically once the unlock time arrives.

5. Game (Sync Pulse)

A quick two-person mini-game played on one shared phone. Both people tap their own half of the screen at the same moment after a countdown, and the app shows how in sync you are as a percentage.
