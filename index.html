A free, no-signup, browser-based Yes/No love prank and interactive proposal website.

What Is "Do You Love Me?"

"Do You Love Me?" is a fun, interactive love prank website where the No button always dodges away — making Yes the only possible answer. It's designed purely for laughter, not as a real love test.

How it works:

  • The recipient opens your shared link
  • They see a "Do You Love Me?" question with Yes/No buttons
  • When they try to press No, the button jumps to a random new spot on the screen
  • Yes is the only button that can ever be clicked
  • Perfect for pranking a crush, girlfriend, boyfriend, or friend

Share instantly via WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or any app — no download required.

✨ 5 Free Advanced Tools (No Signup Needed)

Open "Advanced Options" to unlock five extra relationship mini-tools. Every tool generates a shareable link — no account or app download needed.

1. A & Z Editor (All-in-One Builder)

2. Custom Love Prank

Add your own name and write a custom question (instead of the default "Do you love me?"). When the recipient taps Yes, your personalized name and question are revealed to them.

3. Two-Way Love Decoder

A compatibility quiz for two people:

  • You answer a set of fun questions
  • Send the link to someone else

4. Time Capsule Confession

Write a message today and lock it until a future date and time you choose. Anyone who opens the link early sees only a countdown timer — the message reveals itself automatically once the unlock time arrives.

5. Sync Pulse (Two-Person Mini-Game)

A quick game played on one shared phone. Both people tap their half of the screen at the same moment after a countdown. The app shows how in sync you are as a percentage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the No button work?

The moment you hover, touch, or click No, it instantly jumps to a new random spot on the screen — so it can never actually be caught.

Is this a real love test?

No. It's purely for fun. There is no real love detection — only a lighthearted prank meant to make someone smile.

Do I need to sign up or download an app?

No. Every feature, including all five Advanced Options, works directly in your browser with no account, login, or app store download.

Is this site free to use?

Yes. The main Yes/No prank and all five Advanced Options are completely free, with no in-app purchases or hidden paywalls.
