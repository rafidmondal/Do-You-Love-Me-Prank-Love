Do You Love Me? is a free, interactive love and friendship website where you can create personalized pranks, share secret messages, play couple games, and design beautiful 3D greeting cards. No account, no login, and no app installation needed.

💗 More Than Just a Love Prank

- Yes/No Love Prank: Send someone a link and watch the No button run away every time they try to press it. Yes is the only answer they can choose!
- Custom Love Prank: Add your name, write your own question, and create a personalized surprise.
- Two-Way Love Decoder: Answer six fun questions separately and discover your compatibility score together.
- Time Capsule Confession: Lock a personal message until a future date. The recipient sees a countdown before the message unlocks.
- Sync Pulse: Share one phone, tap together after a countdown, and see how closely your timing matches.
- A & Z Editor: Combine custom questions, themes, background music, secret answers, and time-locked messages into one experience.
- Raxzen Card Studio: Create personalized 3D cards with photos, custom messages, elegant fonts, glowing themes, animations, and music.

✨ Create Something Personal

Choose from themes such as Liquid Glass Pink, Royal Gold, Emerald Glow, Cyberpunk Love, Midnight Ruby, and Sapphire Dream. Customize colors, typography, names, photos, and messages to make each creation feel unique.

Your finished cards can be saved as PNG or JPEG, exported as PDF, printed, or shared. You can also add music and create memorable digital greetings for special occasions.

💬 Your Built-In AI Chat Assistant

Need help using the website? Ask the built-in AI/SI chat about features, link creation, sharing, troubleshooting, romantic messages, friendship, or general website questions. It responds in your language with short, friendly, natural replies.

Chat history stays in your browser, while messages submitted for AI responses are sent to third-party AI providers. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

🔒 Free, Simple & Privacy-Focused

- No signup, login, subscription, or hidden paywall.
- Works directly in your browser on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Generated prank and tool content is encoded in shareable links rather than stored in a Raxzen database.
- Card creation is designed to work locally on your device without a dedicated upload or sharing server.
- You can optionally add the website to your home screen for quicker access.

Important: Anyone who receives a complete shareable link may be able to see the information encoded in it. Keep sensitive messages and personal details out of shared links. The love prank is entertainment, not a genuine way to measure someone's feelings.
